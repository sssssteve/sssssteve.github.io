A Plug-in Framework for Utilizing Tactile Envelope Feedback in RL Post-Training of VLA Policies

What happened at contact?

Images and language can guide a policy toward an object. Once contact begins, tactile changes provide another source of feedback. We study how tactile envelopes can inform reinforcement-learning post-training for manipulation.

Method and evaluation

A tactile expert learns an action-conditioned range of short-horizon contact loads from successful demonstrations. During execution, deviations between measured load and the predicted range are converted into rewards for online policy updates.
