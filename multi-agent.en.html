Multi-agent UAV control

Experiment design ↓
Undergraduate project · Nov 2024–Jan 2025

How can several UAVs avoid obstacles and plan coordinated paths on a simulated 2D grid?

From expert demonstrations to a joint policy

I helped build the dynamic-obstacle environment. A* paths supplied expert demonstrations for offline imitation pretraining, followed by a CNN-DQN policy for coordination.

Experimental comparisons

We varied map size, obstacle density, start positions and destinations. Rewards accounted for first visits, distance to goal and step count, and the project explored meta-learning to adjust reward parameters.
