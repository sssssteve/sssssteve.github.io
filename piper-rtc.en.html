What happens between predictions?

A JAX server hosts the policy while a Piper client streams observations and executes bimanual actions. The project implements two ways to handle discontinuities between successive predictions.

MethodExecution rule
RTCRetain the previous plan and constrain the next chunk in time.
Temporal EnsembleAlign overlapping predictions for a control instant and take a weighted average.
