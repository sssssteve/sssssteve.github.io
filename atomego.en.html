Exploring Ego–Robot Integration for Embodied Foundation Model Pretraining

What transfers from people to robots?

Hands and robot joints use different action representations, but both demonstrations show how objects move. As second author, I worked on our comparison of separate action heads, staged embodiment alignment, and joint video–action prediction.

Real-robot evaluation

Each checkpoint was tested on seven tasks, with ten ID and ten OOD trials per task. Progressive alignment produced the strongest VLA result; adding human data to the WAM route did not yield the same benefit.
