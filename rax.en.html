RAX reproduction demo

What I reproduced

I worked on the observation and tool-calling loop on Piper and Franka. The model reads camera views and a task instruction, then writes a program that calls robot tools. The workbench lets me review the program, execute it separately and inspect its feedback.

A debugging example

During screw placement, the gripper and part could look aligned in the main camera but differ in depth. I compared the side view and checked localization, calibration and action execution. Keeping the program and observations together helped identify which step to adjust on the next attempt.
