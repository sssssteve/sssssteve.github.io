Three training routes

Atom-0 is the team codebase for AtomEgo. It turns three approaches to ego–robot pretraining into distinct training recipes and includes robot-only baselines.

RouteDesign
Atom-DHA shared backbone with separate action heads for humans and robots.
Atom-CLEgo pretraining, embodiment alignment, robot pretraining and downstream Piper adaptation.
Atom-WAMJointly predict future video and actions.
