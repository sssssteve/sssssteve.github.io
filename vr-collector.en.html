Open-source project

From hand motion to demonstration

The operator drives two simulated arms with WebXR controllers. A workstation runs Isaac Sim, renders cameras and records HDF5 episodes. A second input mode reads motion from a pair of Piper leader arms.

After recording

The desktop workflow checks decodable video and state–action alignment and records warnings. Accepted episodes can be exported to the official RoboDojo HDF5 layout.
