PhD research interests

RUA · Robot-Use Agents

With a robot-use agent (RUA), the immediate question is what to do next. If the gripper misses, can the agent inspect another camera view, decide where to move, call the right tool and verify the result? Reproducing RAX lets me compare each generated command with what the arm actually did.

Watch the RAX demo →

RSI · Recursive Self-Improvement

With recursive self-improvement (RSI), I want to study what changes between trials. Can an agent review a failed grasp, revise its observation routine or action program, and test the revision? I would compare both versions on matched layouts, then check whether the improvement carries over to unseen layouts.
