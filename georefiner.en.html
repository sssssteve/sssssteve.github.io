GeoRefiner

Paper ↗

Geometry-Conditioned Plug-and-Play Action Refinement for Vision-Language-Action Models

Refinement after the base policy

A base VLA proposes an action sequence. GeoRefiner uses scene geometry, the instruction and the proposed motion to predict residual corrections. The module operates downstream of a frozen base policy.

Across base models

Staged training establishes geometric representations, trains the refiner and adapts it to additional policies. The paper evaluates multiple backbones and environmental perturbations.
