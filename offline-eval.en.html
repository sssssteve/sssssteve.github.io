What is held constant?

The evaluator inspects an Atom0 Unified80 checkpoint before dispatching to the matching runtime. It supports single- and dual-head π₀.₅ and FastWAM, with explicit action spaces, camera mappings and normalization.

Why separate action errors and flow loss?

The dual-head π₀.₅ action sampler selects the robot head. On EgoVerse, the evaluator computes the supported flow loss so that a robot prediction is never scored as an ego-hand action.
