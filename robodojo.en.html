cuRobo v2 · Simulated manipulation

Turning plans in the Isaac Sim RoboDojo environment into demonstrations that can be inspected after execution.

Block stacking at 25 fps. From left to right: head, left wrist and right wrist.

From plan to recording

I used cuRobo v2 to generate trajectories for RoboDojo tasks. Each attempt retains an HDF5 record, multiview video, the task outcome and integrity checks so the planned motion can be compared with its effect on the object.

Inspecting a trajectory

The overhead view shows the block positions; wrist views reveal the approach, grasp and release. The task verdict and camera streams help check completion, recording integrity and suitability for later training.
