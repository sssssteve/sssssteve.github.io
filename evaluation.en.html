Real-robot case study · AtomEgo

Across seven tasks, the team ran 70 in-domain and 70 out-of-distribution trials per checkpoint. The latter kept the task goal fixed while varying object positions, appearance or physical properties.

The reported best Atom-CL checkpoint completed 30 of 70 out-of-distribution trials; the robot-only VLA baseline completed 16.
